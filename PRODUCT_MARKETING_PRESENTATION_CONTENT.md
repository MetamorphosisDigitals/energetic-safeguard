## Slide 2

### A steady rhythm across the day

- Morning Check-In: arrive, orient, and set a gentle direction.
- Energy Protection: create a little more room around attention before an interaction.
- End-of-Day Cleaning: mark a supportive transition out of the day.

## Slide 3

### Built from 18 meaningful rituals

- A structured, confirmed ritual library supports diverse moments and preferences.
- Users can choose by time, setting, style, and what feels present.
- Rose Rays and crystals remain optional symbolic supports, never requirements.

## Slide 4

### A 21-day rhythm without pressure

- Opening the Daily Routine tracks a gentle calendar-day return.
- Day 7 recognizes a first week of showing up.
- Day 14 acknowledges a growing rhythm; Day 21 celebrates a new habit taking shape.

## Slide 5

### Reflection keeps the routine personal

- Optional day notes capture what felt supportive.
- A Day 7 reflection brings together selected ritual, check-ins, and insights.
- Completed plans can be repeated, adapted, printed, or kept for later reference.

## Slide 6

### Safety and choice shape every path

- Language is consent-based and never forceful.
- A guarded selection flow pauses before a ritual when more support or orientation is appropriate.
- Symbolic language stays optional; the experience makes no diagnosis or treatment claims.

## Slide 7

### Privacy is an active choice

- Active routines, notes, and reflections are local to the user’s device by default.
- Signed-in users may manually back up completed plans or enable automatic backup for future completed plans.
- Each cloud copy can be restored, labeled, pinned, searched, or deleted independently.

- Daily Routine setup and cloud backup are invitations, not requirements.
