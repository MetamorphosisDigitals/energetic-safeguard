# Chosen Direction — Soft Sovereignty

## Design Movement
**Contemporary apothecary editorialism.** The app combines the tactile reassurance of an independent wellness journal with the clarity of a modern care tool. It avoids mystical visual clichés while leaving room for optional symbolic Rose Ray practices.

## Core Principles
1. **Choice before instruction:** layouts present a calm, clear next step while foregrounding agency and options.
2. **Soft structure:** warm paper surfaces, fine botanical lines, and restrained panels create containment without visual pressure.
3. **Readable ritual:** generous type, concise prompts, and single-task screens ensure that the experience stays usable in moments of overwhelm.
4. **Evidence-aware symbolism:** the visual language supports spiritual preference as a personal layer, never as a medical claim.

## Color Philosophy
Warm Cream is the expansive, non-clinical base. Deep Plum provides readable grounding and should only appear in strong type, navigation, and primary actions. Dusty Rose signals gentleness; lavender marks supportive choices; Soft Gold is reserved for moments of progress, personal capacity, and the subtle “one clear step” indicator. These colors should feel like pigment on tactile paper, not like a gradient-heavy wellness template.

## Layout Paradigm
The home experience uses a **journal cover and field-note** composition: a quiet brand masthead beside one dominant “right now” prompt, followed by an uneven procession of pathways. Cards are intentionally staggered with varied interior composition rather than arranged as an undifferentiated, centered grid. Individual flows read like sequential cards in a small private notebook.

## Signature Elements
1. **Rose compass:** a compact eight-petal line symbol used for the brand mark, loading moments, and active progress.
2. **Field-note tab:** a vertical or horizontal paper tab with small uppercase labels such as “FOR THIS MOMENT” or “YOUR PRACTICE.”
3. **Pressed-stem lines:** exceptionally light botanical curve patterns that frame major panels and do not compete with content.

## Interaction Philosophy
Controls feel steady and immediate, with one clear primary action on every screen and plain-language exits such as “Stop for now.” Selection states use color and subtle shape change, never alarming status colors. The interface validates decisions without asserting outcomes.

## Animation
Cards can enter on a 180–240ms opacity-and-translate transition with 40ms stagger. Primary buttons compress to 0.97 scale on press. The rose compass may rotate no more than 15 degrees during pathway transitions. All animation is disabled for reduced-motion preferences; practice navigation is instant when reduced motion is active.

## Typography System
Use **Fraunces** for the brand name, page titles, and a small number of reflective phrases; its soft, editorial serif brings warmth without sacrificing legibility. Use **Manrope** for controls, questions, body copy, and instructions. Headings are semibold rather than oversized; labels are uppercase Manrope with deliberate tracking; instructions stay at a comfortable 16px minimum.

## Brand Essence
**A private, choice-led guide for sensitive people who want a grounding practice that fits the moment.**

Personality: **steady, discerning, tender.**

## Brand Voice
Headlines are direct, compassionate, and unhurried. CTAs name the next action rather than making a promise. Microcopy is practical first and symbolic only when selected.

Example lines:

> “Let’s find one small way to come back to yourself.”

> “You can pause here. Your body gets to choose what feels supportive.”

## Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark pairs a high-contrast “Energetic” with a quieter “Safeguard” in the Fraunces family. The mark is an open eight-petal rose compass with a sheltered central point, suggesting orientation and boundaries rather than a literal flower.

## Signature Brand Color
**Sanctuary Plum — #4D314B.** A deep, anchored plum used to create trust, definition, and visual calm.
