# Moon Rituals Card Review

## Recommendation

**Add a Moon Rituals card, but do not add the supplied moon and eclipse practices as new selectable rituals.** The confirmed 18-ritual catalog should remain the app's sole runtime selection library. The supplied material is strongest as an optional, symbolic **Moon Cycle content layer** that selects an existing grounded ritual and then adds moon-specific framing, journaling prompts, optional Rose Ray imagery, and optional crystal symbolism.

This approach preserves the app's deterministic 18-ritual architecture while giving the new card a distinct purpose: users choose a moon moment for reflection, not a different clinical, predictive, or fear-based experience.

## Recommended Card

| Dashboard placement | Title | Card purpose | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **First card above the five current pathways** | **Moon Rituals** | A reflective, optional moon-cycle doorway for intention, release, pause, or integration. | **Choose a moon moment** |

The card should use a moon icon, a calm midnight-plum tint, and copy such as: **“Use the moon as a gentle cue to reflect, release, pause, or begin again—without pressure or prediction.”** It should not claim to detect the current lunar phase automatically in the first release. Users should choose the moment that feels relevant.

## Four Recommended Moon Moments

| Moon moment | Source themes | Existing canonical ritual | Optional layer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **New Moon — Set a gentle intention** | Sacred Seed, rest, compassionate beginning | **Feet, Breath, Intention** | Emerald or Pink Rose imagery; one realistic intention and one practical action. |
| **Full Moon — Notice and release** | Gratitude, completion, letting go | **End-of-Day Release** | Violet Rose symbolism; journal prompt about what can be set down without forcing release. |
| **Solar Eclipse — Pause and protect your energy** | Sovereign Pause, discernment, protection | **Five-Sense Orientation** | Optional Emerald Rose grounding; explicit quiet-pause framing and normal eye-safety guidance if outdoors. |
| **Lunar Eclipse — Feel, reflect, and integrate** | Emotional witness, compassion, relationship discernment | **Daily Boundary Question** | Optional Pink or Blue Rose imagery; reflection prompt and an eyes-open grounding close. |

## Why This Fits

The source material already uses many practices aligned with the product's principles: natural breathing, optional imagery, ordinary environmental grounding, one small practical action, and language that permits pausing or adapting. It also explicitly rejects urgency, fear, and dramatic interpretation. Those elements fit the existing consent-based design and the catalog's optional Rose Ray and crystal supports.

> “Sacred ritual should leave you more grounded, not more frightened; more sovereign, not more dependent on the sky for permission to trust yourself.”

The card should therefore be a **reflection and modifier flow**, not a lunar prediction tool or a spiritual requirement. The 16 longer scripts may later be curated as optional guided-script variants inside a Moon Cycle content pack, but they should not enlarge the selectable ritual count.

## Safeguards Required Before Implementation

| Area | Proposed guardrail |
| --- | --- |
| **Safety** | Route the selected canonical ritual through the existing safety-aware selector before a practice renders. Urgent, high-intensity, transit, and malformed-input handoffs remain unchanged. |
| **Language** | Keep all moon, Rose Ray, and crystal references optional and symbolic; avoid certainty, prediction, or pressure to release, manifest, decide, or interpret. |
| **Accessibility** | Offer eyes-open and non-visual alternatives for every image-based prompt; keep movement, lying down, candles, and physical crystals optional. |
| **Eclipse content** | Include the supplied practical warning not to look at the Sun without certified eclipse protection. Do not provide astronomical prediction or location-specific eclipse advice. |
| **Emotional content** | Keep grief, shadow, relationship, and cord material as optional reflection prompts with orientation and practical-support exits; avoid treatment or trauma-processing claims. |
| **Data governance** | Store the moon moment as a route modifier or script variant, not as a nineteenth selectable ritual. |

## Proposed First Release Scope

The first release should include the one dashboard card, four manually chosen moon moments, their existing canonical ritual mapping, and a short optional reflection prompt per moment. It should defer automatic lunar-calendar matching, eclipse timing, location data, and the full 16 long-form scripts.

This provides a useful, distinctive experience without overloading the home dashboard or weakening the already validated catalog and safety model.
