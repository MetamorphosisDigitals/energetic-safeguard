## Launch Copy Guardrails

All channels should keep the product voice grounded and consent-led. Avoid claiming clinical outcomes, diagnosis, treatment, guaranteed transformation, or that a ritual is required for safety. Describe cloud backup as optional and user-controlled, and describe the 21-day point as a product celebration of a habit taking shape rather than a universal scientific threshold.
