# The Energetic Safeguard: Landing Page Copy and Onboarding Outline

## Brand Foundation

**Product name:** The Energetic Safeguard  
**Tagline:** *Ground your energy. Protect your peace. Return to yourself.*

The voice is warm, steady, and choice-led. It avoids diagnosis, pressure, fear-based claims, and promises of transformation. It describes rituals as optional supports rather than requirements, and it treats cloud backup as an explicit account choice rather than an invisible default.

## First-Time-User Onboarding Flow

The onboarding flow should be concise, choice-led, and should never require a user to disclose sensitive information. It introduces support before asking for commitment, then offers Daily Routine setup as an optional next step.

| Step | Screen title | Purpose | Key copy | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Welcome to The Energetic Safeguard** | Establish emotional tone and scope. | “Ground your energy. Protect your peace. Return to yourself.” | **Continue** |
| 2 | **Support for the moment you are in** | Explain the app’s practical value. | “Choose a short ritual for beginning, protection, grounding, transition, or release.” | **See how it works** |
| 3 | **You choose the pace** | Establish consent and optionality. | “Nothing here needs to be perfect. Choose what feels possible today.” | **Choose your style** |
| 4 | **Choose a practice style** | Capture preference without sensitive data. | “Would you prefer practical, visual, or symbolic support?” | **Save my preference** |
| 5 | **Set up a Daily Routine** *(optional)* | Invite the three-part rhythm. | “Choose a Morning Check-In, Energy Protection, and End-of-Day Cleaning ritual—or begin with one practice today.” | **Build my routine** / **Not now** |
| 6 | **Keep your completed plans** *(signed-in prompt, optional)* | Explain explicit cloud backup. | “Sign in if you would like to back up completed plans to your account. Your active routine remains on this device unless you choose backup.” | **Sign in to back up** / **Keep using this device** |

## Key Value Propositions for First-Time Users

**A clear next step when the day feels full.** Instead of asking people to design their own practice from scratch, the app helps them choose a grounded starting point.

**A routine that is personal, not prescriptive.** Users choose their morning, protection, and evening rituals, and can revise the rhythm as their needs change.

**Consistency without pressure.** The 7-, 14-, and 21-day markers acknowledge return and practice without framing a missed day as failure.

**Privacy and control by design.** Notes, reflections, and routine history stay local by default. Cloud backup is optional, explicit, and manageable plan by plan.

**Support that respects boundaries.** The experience uses consent-based language and safety-aware routing. Symbolic supports can be meaningful, but they are never necessary to participate.
