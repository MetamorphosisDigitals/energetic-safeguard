# The Energetic Safeguard: Day 21 Retention Email Sequence

## Sequence Strategy

The sequence begins only after a user reaches the 21-day Daily Routine milestone. Its purpose is to recognize a quiet accomplishment, offer a useful next step, and invite feedback without turning the milestone into an obligation. It does not use fear of loss, streak shaming, urgency, or a paywall around rituals.

| Email | Timing | Purpose | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. A habit is taking shape | Day 21, after the milestone | Celebrate and invite reflection | Review my 21-day rhythm |
| 2. Choose what comes next | Three days later | Offer repeat, adapt, or lighter-rhythm choices | Choose my next step |
| 3. Keep what supported you | Seven days later | Invite optional feedback and optional cloud continuity | Share feedback |

## Email 1 — A Habit Is Taking Shape

**Send timing:** Day 21, after the in-app milestone appears.

**Subject line options:**

1. A new habit is taking shape
2. Twenty-one days of returning to yourself
3. Your Daily Routine has made a little more room

**Preheader:** Notice what supported you. Keep what still fits.

**Headline:** A new habit is taking shape.

You have opened your Daily Routine for 21 days. That does not have to mean perfection. It can simply mean that you have made a small, steady place to return to yourself.

Take a moment to notice what felt useful: a morning check-in, a little more room around an interaction, or an evening ritual that helped the day have an ending.

Your completed plan is ready when you are. You can review your notes, print a private summary, repeat the same rhythm, or try something different.

**Primary CTA:** Review my 21-day rhythm  
**Secondary CTA:** Return to today’s ritual

**Footer note:** All guided rituals remain available whenever you need them.

## Email 2 — Choose What Comes Next

**Send timing:** Three days after Email 1, only if the user has not already started a new plan.

**Subject line options:**

1. What would support your next rhythm?
2. Keep the ritual, or let it change
3. Your next small return can look different

**Preheader:** Repeat, adapt, or keep a lighter rhythm—your choice.

**Headline:** Choose what comes next.

The routine that supported you for the last 21 days can stay the same, become lighter, or change with the season you are in.

You might repeat the plan that felt familiar. You might choose a different Morning Check-In, Energy Protection, or End-of-Day Cleaning ritual. Or you might simply keep one ritual close and return when it feels useful.

There is no required next step. The purpose is to keep what works and release what does not.

**Primary CTA:** Choose my next step  
**Secondary CTA:** Explore a different ritual

## Email 3 — Keep What Supported You

**Send timing:** Seven days after Email 1, only if the user has not submitted the Day 21 survey.

**Subject line options:**

1. What made your rhythm feel supportive?
2. A quick question about your 21-day routine
3. Help us make the next return clearer

**Preheader:** Share only what you are comfortable sharing. Every question is optional.

**Headline:** What made your rhythm feel supportive?

If you have a minute, we would be grateful to hear what felt clear, what felt difficult, and what would make the next return more useful. The feedback survey is optional, and you can skip any question.

If you would like to keep your completed plan available across devices, you can also choose to back it up to your account. Cloud backup is optional; your active routine remains on your device unless you choose otherwise.

**Primary CTA:** Share feedback  
**Secondary CTA:** Review backup options

## Lifecycle Rules

The sequence should stop when a user unsubscribes from lifecycle messages, completes the survey, or starts a new 21-day plan. It should not be sent to users who have explicitly paused routine reminders. Any account-based segmentation should use only routine milestone and product-action metadata; it should not inspect the content of private notes or reflections.

The language should remain consistent with the app: it recognizes return rather than compliance, offers a choice rather than an instruction, and makes clear that all guided rituals remain available without a subscription.
