# The Energetic Safeguard: Day 21 Feedback Survey

## Survey Purpose

This short, optional survey is shown after a user reaches the 21-day Daily Routine milestone. It is designed to improve the product experience without asking users to disclose health status, diagnoses, trauma, crisis information, or private reflection content. The survey should never block access to a ritual, completed plan, or cloud-history control.

## Intro Screen Copy

**Title:** A rhythm is taking shape.

**Body:** You have returned to your Daily Routine for 21 days. If you would like, tell us what made the experience more—or less—supportive. Your answers are optional, and you can skip every question.

**Privacy note:** Please do not include private health information, sensitive personal details, or anything you would not want used to improve the product.

**Actions:** **Share feedback** / **Not now**

## Core Survey Questions

| # | Question | Response type | Answer choices / instructions | Product use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | How supported did you feel by your Daily Routine? | Five-point scale | Not at all supported → Very supported | Measures routine-level value. |
| 2 | Which part of the routine did you return to most often? | Single select | Morning Check-In; Energy Protection; End-of-Day Cleaning; A mix of all three; None in particular | Informs default emphasis and content discovery. |
| 3 | How easy was it to choose a ritual? | Five-point scale | Very difficult → Very easy | Identifies selection friction. |
| 4 | Did the 7-, 14-, and 21-day markers feel helpful? | Single select | Yes, helpful; Neutral; Not helpful; I did not notice them | Tests milestone relevance without asking for emotional disclosure. |
| 5 | What would you most like to do next? | Single select | Repeat this routine; Try a different ritual; Keep a lighter rhythm; Explore my completed plans; I am not sure yet | Guides next-step personalization. |
| 6 | If you used plan backup, how clear did it feel? | Conditional five-point scale | Very unclear → Very clear; Not applicable | Evaluates optional cloud continuity. |
| 7 | What should we improve first? | Single select | Ritual choice; Daily Routine; Reminders; Plan history; Cloud backup; Accessibility; Something else | Prioritizes product work. |
| 8 | Is there anything you would like us to know? | Optional short text | “Share only product feedback. Please do not include sensitive personal information.” | Captures qualitative product feedback. |

## Completion Screen Copy

**Title:** Thank you for sharing.

**Body:** Your feedback helps us make the experience clearer, calmer, and more useful. You can return to a ritual, repeat your plan, or simply leave the app here.

**Primary action:** Return to my routine  
**Secondary action:** View completed plans

## Implementation Notes

The survey should be stored separately from personal practice notes and cloud-plan content. It should be detached from the text of any routine reflection by default, and it should be possible to submit without a signed-in account. If an account is used, any analysis should aggregate product-feedback fields rather than expose an individual’s note text to routine-history views.

The survey should appear once after a completed 21-day cycle and remain dismissible. If a user skips it, the product may offer it again only after a materially new cycle or a user-initiated feedback action—not as repeated pressure after every app opening.
