THE PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
06

Safety and choice shape every path.

Support should make more room for agency—not less.

No diagnosis. No treatment claims. No forced commands.
01

Consent-led language

Every ritual is offered as an invitation. Users choose their pace, their practice, and whether symbolic support belongs in the moment.

02

Guarded selection

When an input calls for more caution, the experience pauses before showing a ritual and offers a supportive orientation instead.

03

Optional symbolism

Rose Rays and crystals can enrich a ritual for users who want them. Nothing physical or symbolic is required to participate.
