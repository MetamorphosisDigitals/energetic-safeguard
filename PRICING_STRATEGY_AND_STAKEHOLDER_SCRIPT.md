# The Energetic Safeguard: Pricing Strategy and Stakeholder Presentation Script

## Pricing Principle

> **The ritual itself is never paywalled.** Users can access every guided ritual, safety-aware recommendation, basic Daily Routine, and local private reflection without payment.

The commercial model should charge for **continuity, convenience, and deeper organization**, rather than restricting support in a difficult moment. This is aligned with the current product promise: the 18 guided rituals remain unrestricted, while optional account-based continuity and advanced habit tools create a sustainable paid layer.

## Proposed Subscription Structure

| Tier | Proposed price | Intended user | Included value |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Grounded** | **Free** | Anyone seeking moment-to-moment support | All 18 guided rituals; safety-aware recommendations; three-part Daily Routine; local notes and reflections; 7-, 14-, and 21-day routine milestones; printable local summaries. |
| **Continuity** | **US$3.99/month** or **US$29/year** | Users who want their completed plans available across devices | Everything in Grounded, plus optional cloud backup, restore to a new device, cloud plan history, plan labels, pinning, search, and individual cloud-copy deletion. |
| **Rhythm Plus** | **US$6.99/month** or **US$49/year** | Users building a more intentional ongoing practice | Everything in Continuity, plus advanced habit insights, extended plan archive analytics, additional export formats, richer routine organization, and future advanced habit features as they are released. |

The annual options provide a clear value choice without making support contingent on payment. At launch, the product could keep the purchase surface simple by offering only **Grounded** and **Continuity**, then introduce **Rhythm Plus** when its advanced habit features are fully available and clearly differentiated.

## Feature Boundary

| Remains unrestricted | Optional paid continuity and advanced habit value |
| --- | --- |
| All guided rituals and safety handoffs | Cloud backup and cross-device restoration |
| Recommendation pathways and One-Minute Reset | Searchable cloud archive and organized cloud plans |
| Daily Routine and 7/14/21-day markers | Extended archive analytics and richer exports |
| Local notes, reflections, and printable local plan summaries | Future advanced habit features and account-based continuity |

## Conversion Moments

The product should not lead with an upgrade prompt during a ritual or immediately after a safety-oriented handoff. The best conversion moments are after a user has completed a seven-day plan, after they choose to protect a completed plan, or when they attempt to restore history on a different device.

| Moment | Recommended message | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| After Day 7 reflection | “Keep this plan available when you need it.” | Explore Continuity |
| After a completed plan | “Back up this completed plan to your account by choice.” | Back up this plan |
| On a new device | “Your completed plans can travel with you.” | Restore from cloud |
| In cloud history | “Organize a lasting record of the routines that supported you.” | Explore Rhythm Plus |

## Guardrails

The upgrade language should remain consistent with the product’s consent-based voice. It should never imply that someone must pay to access grounding, protection, emergency support, or safety features. Backup must remain explicitly opt-in, the automatic-backup preference must remain user-controlled, and cloud plan deletion must remain available at the plan level.

# Stakeholder Presentation Script

## Slide 1 — The Product in One Sentence

**Speaker script:** “The Energetic Safeguard is a mobile-first wellness and spiritual-support app that helps people choose one grounded next ritual for the moment they are in. Its promise is simple: ground your energy, protect your peace, and return to yourself.”

## Slide 2 — The Problem We Address

**Speaker script:** “People do not usually need more options when a day feels full. They need one clear, supportive next step. The app responds to that need with short, choice-led rituals for mornings, interactions, transitions, overload, and end-of-day release.”

## Slide 3 — The Ritual Library

**Speaker script:** “The experience is built on a confirmed library of 18 rituals. The user can arrive through a pathway, available time, setting, style preference, or a Daily Routine slot. Rose Rays and crystals can add personal meaning, but they are always optional and never required.”

## Slide 4 — The Three-Part Daily Routine

**Speaker script:** “Daily Routine turns momentary support into a personal rhythm. Each user chooses a Morning Check-In, an Energy Protection ritual, and an End-of-Day Cleaning ritual. These selections are flexible and can evolve as the user’s life changes.”

## Slide 5 — 7, 14, and 21-Day Milestones

**Speaker script:** “The habit experience is intentionally gentle. Opening the routine tracks a calendar-day return. At seven days, we recognize the first week; at fourteen, a growing rhythm; and at twenty-one, we celebrate a new habit taking shape. The language never treats a missed day as failure.”

## Slide 6 — Reflection and Personal History

**Speaker script:** “Users can add optional day notes, complete a Day 7 reflection, repeat or adapt a plan, and print a summary. This makes the routine a record of what felt supportive, not just a counter of what was completed.”

## Slide 7 — Safety and Privacy

**Speaker script:** “Safety and agency shape the product. The recommendation system uses guarded selection and can pause before showing a ritual when a more supportive orientation is appropriate. Local notes and active routines stay on the device by default, and all product language is consent-based.”

## Slide 8 — Optional Cloud Continuity

**Speaker script:** “For users who want continuity, completed plans can be backed up to a signed-in account by choice. They can back up manually or opt into automatic backup for future completed plans. Cloud copies can be restored, labeled, pinned, searched, and deleted individually.”

## Slide 9 — Monetization That Respects Support

**Speaker script:** “Our pricing principle is that no one should pay to access a ritual in a moment of need. All 18 guided rituals remain unrestricted. The paid opportunity is continuity and advanced organization: secure cloud history, cross-device restoration, richer plan management, and future advanced habit insights.”

## Slide 10 — The Growth Logic

**Speaker script:** “The user first experiences real value through free rituals and their local Daily Routine. The natural upgrade moment appears after a completed plan, at restoration on another device, or when someone wants a durable, organized history. This makes conversion contextual, optional, and aligned with the product’s trust model.”

## Slide 11 — Closing

**Speaker script:** “The Energetic Safeguard combines immediate support with a rhythm that can grow over time. It is designed to feel personal, private, and practical—one small ritual at a time.”
