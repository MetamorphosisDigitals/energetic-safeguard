SYSTEM OF RECORD

One source of truth for each concern.

The architecture keeps payment collection, projected access, and user experience intentionally separate.

01 · BROWSER

Chooses & displays

Selects a plan, opens checkout, and reads the application’s projected entitlement status.

02 · APP SERVER

Verifies & projects

Creates Checkout, verifies events, maps customers, and writes the feature-access projection.

03 · STRIPE

Collects & signals

Hosts payment collection, manages subscriptions, and sends lifecycle events.

04 · DATABASE

Remembers & audits

Stores customer mapping, entitlement state, event outcomes, and routine-history continuity.

KEY RULE

The browser never interprets a redirect as paid access.
