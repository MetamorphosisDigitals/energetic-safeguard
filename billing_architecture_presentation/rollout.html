PRODUCTION RUNBOOK

Production rollout is deliberately staged.

Every checkpoint verifies the billing boundary before it reaches more people.

CONTROLLED AVAILABILITY
01 · CONFIGURE

Claim & define

Claim the billing account, create Products and Prices, and add live secrets through the project secret manager.

02 · SANDBOX

Sign & simulate

Register the HTTPS webhook endpoint, forward sandbox events, and test signature and lifecycle projection.

03 · OBSERVE

Watch without selling

Deploy the event ledger and reconciliation report in observe-only mode before enabling Checkout.

04 · COHORT

Start small

Enable Checkout for internal accounts or a controlled cohort; monitor mismatch, latency, and support signals.

05 · EXPAND

Broaden with evidence

Enable availability only after events, projections, reconciliation, and customer paths behave as intended.

LAUNCH GATE

Billing may scale only after its safeguards are proven—and ritual access remains unchanged in every scenario.
